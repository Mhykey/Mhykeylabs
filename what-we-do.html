What We Do

Understanding First. Technology Where It Makes Sense.

Mhykey Labs focuses on understanding businesses, discovering meaningful problems and using technology where it can create practical value.

Business & Organizational Understanding

We seek to understand how businesses actually operate, including their workflows, information, records, processes and everyday decisions.

The goal is not to assume that technology is the answer, but to understand the situation clearly enough to know where technology may or may not help.

Research & Discovery

We conduct research around real businesses and their operations.

We look for recurring problems, how frequently they occur, their consequences, how businesses currently handle them, and where meaningful opportunities may exist.

Systems & Technology

When a real operational need has been established, technology can provide a better way to manage information, processes and workflows.

Our technology work is guided by what the business actually needs rather than by technology for its own sake.

Development Where Justified

Not every problem requires a new software product.

Where research establishes that a purpose-built technology solution is appropriate, development can follow from the understanding already gained.

How We Approach Problems

Understand — Start with the business, its environment and how work currently happens.
Research — Investigate recurring problems, workflows, consequences and existing approaches.
Identify — Separate meaningful opportunities from assumptions and surface-level observations.
Decide — Determine whether technology is actually justified.
Build — Where appropriate, develop a practical solution based on what has been learned.

Technology Should Solve Something Real

Our approach is intentionally simple: understand reality first, then decide what should happen next.

Research does not automatically become a product.
